What is this?
Unlike the Lattice Gas, this is an Off-Lattice Monte Carlo simulation. Particles float freely in continuous space experiencing the real Lennard-Jones intermolecular forces (attraction at distance, repulsion when overlapping).

The Phase Diagram: The diagram now represents mapping of Density (ρ*) to Temperature (T*). Setting the exact dot into the domains of gas-liquid coexistence will cause the system to physically separate into a dense floating droplet entirely surrounded by empty vacuum!

Visual Color Scheme: Particles analyze their local spatial neighbors to guess their phase state:
Purple: Solid-like (Hexagonal packed)
Blue: Liquid-like (Dense fluid)
Grey: Gas-like (Isolated)
