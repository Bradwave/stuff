Simulation Mechanics:
This employs an authentic real-time Molecular Dynamics integrator (Velocity Verlet).

Intramolecular: Stiff harmonic springs uphold covalent O-H bonds (~1Å) and H-O-H angles (104.5°).

Intermolecular: Particles interact via Coulombic electrostatics (simulating polarity gradients) and Lennard-Jones core repulsion (volume exclusion).

Hydrogen Bonds: Electrostatics naturally cause O (neg) and H (pos) to form structural networks (ice lattices) at lower kinetic temperatures!
